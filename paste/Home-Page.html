What I keep coming back to

Above all, wellbeing. It sits at the heart of every system I design and lead.

Capacity before toolsGrowing people matters more than adding platforms. It shows in the PD I run for staff and technicians and the AI literacy sessions I lead for colleagues.
Fair and transparent systemsFairness comes from systems that are open and consistent. I built Compass Point as an equitable route into leadership and an IA moderation tool that keeps marking calibrated.
AI used carefullyI would rather know how AI is really being used than guess. I ran a school-wide usage survey and am turning it into clear guidance and next steps.
Learning measured in skillsWhat students can do matters more than what they recall. It drives my two-platform classroom pilot on skill-building and a map tracking skills across grades 6 to 12.
